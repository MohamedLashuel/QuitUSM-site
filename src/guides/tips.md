These practical tips won't break a social media addiction (see [The QuitUSM Book](/book) for that) but are easy to implement and can help cut down social media use and screen time.

## 1. Ask yourself why

**Whenever you open your phone or computer, ask yourself why you are opening it.** This forces you to be mindful when you would otherwise start scrolling without thinking.

## 2. Set time limits

**Pick certain times of the day as no-screen times**. Choose the times you'd most like to stop screens during, and don't use screens in that time. A popular choice is in the hours before bed. Better yet, pick a time in the day as the only time you can use screens.

## 3. Set space limits

**Pick certain parts of your house where screens are off-limits**. For example, if you often waste time on your phone lying down in your bedroom, then forbid screen use in your bedroom. Better yet, pick a room as the only place where you can use your phone or screens in general.

## 4. Don't have your phone within reach at night

If the first thing you do in the morning is check your phone, **don't keep it within reach of your bed**. If you need your phone's alarm, get a classic alarm clock or at least move your phone to the other side of the room.

## 5. Silence notifications

Each notification can trigger us to pick up our phones again and waste more minutes. **Disable notifications from time-wasting apps unless completely necessary**.

## 6. Set fixed times to check apps

Instead of, for example, checking an app's messages whenever you get a notification or feel like it, **pick times during the day where you can check** - for example, 12:00 P.M., 4:00 P.M., and 8:00 P.M.. Outside of these times, don't open these apps to see what you missed. (If you miss the time, you can still check it, but not again until the next time).

## 7. Monitor screen time

Keeping a close eye on your screen time throughout the day can make you more mindful of how you use your screens. **Set up a screen time reminder** to keep track. iPhone and Android have built-in widgets that display your daily screen time, while computers can use the free app [ActivityWatch](https://activitywatch.net/)

## 8. Grayscale your screens

**Turn your screens black and white**. This makes them less engaging, naturally reducing the time you use them. Most phones and computers should have options to do this built-in.

## 9. Use social media blockers

Social media blockers are apps that restrict access to certain websites or apps. You can block them completely, allow/block them at certain times or days of the week, or simply force yourself to pause for a few seconds before continuing on.

## 10. Replace with other activities

Having a lot of down time makes it more likely you pick up your phone or computer to kill the boredom instead of sitting with it. **Plan out activities in advance to do instead of using screens**, whether that be a hobby, going to events, meeting with friends. or something simple like taking a walk.
