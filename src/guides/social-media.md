---
title: Cutting Back on Social Media
description: Practical steps for loosening the grip of social feeds.
order: 2
---
Social feeds are designed to keep you scrolling. These steps put friction back in the way.

## Remove the apps

Delete social apps from your phone and use them only on a computer, where sessions are naturally shorter.

## Curate what remains

Unfollow accounts that leave you feeling worse. Keep the ones that genuinely connect you with people.

## Schedule your check-ins

Decide in advance when you will check, and for how long.
