# Website Plan

THIS PLAN IS OUTDATED.

## Style

- Primary color: For the first draft, make this grayscale - color will come later

- Font: Verdana

- Small border radius

## Navigation bar

Colored the primary color. Items are the following:

- Text "QuitUSM" in bold

- Home (Home Page)

- About (unlinked for now)

- The QuitUSM Book (unlinked for now)

- Guides (Submenu with all guides)

- Blog (Blog Page)

A simple navbar on desktop, but on mobile it becomes the "QuitUSM" text with a hamburger menu

## Footer

Colored with a dark version of the primary color. On the top-left, a github icon (unlinked for now) and a Discord icon that says "Join our Discord community". Below that, a copyright notice under the MIT license. On the right-hand side, "Contact" in bold, then an email address

## Home Page

On the top sits the image public/images/home_banner.jpg. On the bottom left of the image, there is the headline "QuitUSM" large and unbolded in white, under it "A movement to reclaim our life from screens" in white.

Next, a paragraph.

Then, three CTA cards in a very light version of the primary color. On the left, a large bold headline. Under it, a short description text. On the right, a large Google Material Design icon, and under it a button in the primary color.

On mobile: The icon is at the top, followed by headline, description, then button.

## Guides

When hovered over, expands to a list of all guide pages. Guide pages live at /guides/(slug) and are styled as articles.

On mobile, it instead expands on click.

## Blog

Large headline, article search bar, then cards: 3 columns on desktop and 1 mobile, cards have image, article title in bold, then article description. 6 articles shown by default with a "load more" button

## Article Pages

A large headline. Then, normal text, images, etc whatever is in the article markdown. On larger screens, give a reasonable margin on the sides rather than let text take up the whole screen.
