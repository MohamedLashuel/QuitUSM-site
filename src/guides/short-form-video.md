description: How to step away from endless short videos.

---
Short-form video is engineered for the next swipe. Here is how to reclaim that attention.

## Notice the trigger

Most sessions start from boredom or avoidance. Notice the feeling before you open the app.

## Replace, don't just remove

Have an alternative ready: a book, a walk, or a call to a friend.

## Go grayscale

Switching your phone display to grayscale makes colourful feeds noticeably less compelling.
