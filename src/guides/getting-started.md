Quitting unhealthy screen use starts with noticing it. This guide walks you through your first week.

## Track before you change

For three days, write down every time you pick up your phone without a clear reason. Don't judge it, just note it.

## Pick one change

Choose a single, specific change, such as charging your phone outside the bedroom. Small and consistent beats big and abandoned.

## Tell someone

Share your goal with a friend or with the community. Saying it out loud makes it real.
