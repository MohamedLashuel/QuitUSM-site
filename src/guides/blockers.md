One of the reasons social media takes up so much of our time is because the apps and websites are seconds away at all times. It's too easy to start scrolling on habit or impulse, throwing away time to the void before we've realized what's happened.

Social media blockers solve this problem by restricting access to any websites or apps you specify. This adds a barrier to social media use that deters impulse scrolling. Most allow you to set certain times or days to block - for example, you can restrict access to social media during work hours or weekdays only.

I've personally tested the best social media blockers for every browser and platform, and listed my recommendations below. All are **free** unless specified otherwise - most paid options don't really offer any useful extras (with some exceptions).
