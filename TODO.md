Unplug challenge outline:

- Goal: Inspire people to take a day off of screens mindfully, to get them interested in further changes. Show them they don't need their phone as much as they think, or make them realize how dependent they are

- Hook: Something like, it's hard to believe that decades ago, people lived without the mobile phones that are ubiquitous now

Why:

- We spend so much time on screens and disconnect from the world as a result

- Fear of being without phones

- Shake up your life and learn something new

Details:

- Challenge rules: For 24 hours, no screens (phones, laptops, TVs, etc)

Tips:

- Let people know/arrange plans beforehand

Benefits:

- Connection with the world and loved ones

- Increased productivity
